Journey with the Best is a local travel company specializing in authentic, personalized journeys across Pakistan and Afghanistan. For more than a decade, our team has designed travel for people who want to see the region as it really is — not as a brochure.

Our story

A decade of walking these valleys and cities.

Journey with the Best was founded by experienced local travel experts who have spent more than a decade exploring the region’s breathtaking landscapes, rich cultures, historic cities and hidden destinations.

From the mountains of Hunza and Skardu to the heritage of Punjab and Sindh, and from the historic streets of Afghanistan to its remarkable landscapes, we help travelers discover the region beyond the usual tourist routes.

We don’t believe in generic tour packages. Every journey we design is thoughtfully built around your interests, your pace and your travel style — with a focus on authentic experiences, local connections, responsible travel and reliable support from arrival to departure.

Whether you’re seeking adventure, culture, history or simply a deeper connection with the places you visit, we make every journey personal and memorable.

What drives us

Mission, vision and the values we won’t compromise on.

Mission

Travel that honors the place.

To design private, personalized journeys that help travelers experience the real culture, history, food, people and landscapes of Pakistan and Afghanistan — while supporting the communities that make these regions worth visiting.

Vision

The region, as it really is.

To be the trusted local partner for travelers who want to see the region beyond the headlines — and to show the world a Pakistan and Afghanistan that is warm, rich, generous and extraordinary.

Values

Authenticity, honesty, care.

Every itinerary is designed around you, every price is transparent, every detail is handled by people on the ground. We only propose journeys we would travel ourselves.

How we design journeys

The quiet craft behind every itinerary.

Great travel is a hundred small decisions made well. This is how we make them.

  1. 01

    We listen first

    Before we propose anything, we understand who’s traveling, what calls you to the region, and the pace that suits you. Routes come after the conversation, not before.

  2. 02

    We design, not template

    Every journey is written from scratch — the regions, the stays, the guides, the meals, the drives. No fixed group departures, no generic circuits.

  3. 03

    We stay local

    Our ground team lives in these valleys and cities. The guides who meet you grew up in the places they’ll show you. The hospitality is real because the people are home.

  4. 04

    We travel responsibly

    We work with family-run stays, local drivers and small operators where we can. Your money stays close to the places you’re visiting.

  5. 05

    We are there, start to finish

    One point of contact from your first enquiry to the ride home. If something changes on the road — weather, flights, interests — we adjust in real time.

Where we operate

Two countries we know intimately.

We design journeys across the breadth of Pakistan and the key regions of Afghanistan — and we know which combinations work and which don’t.

Pakistan

  • Gilgit-Baltistan — Hunza, Attabad, Khunjerab, Nagar
  • Skardu & Karakoram — Shigar, Deosai, K2 base-camp
  • Punjab — Lahore, Multan, Harappa
  • Islamabad & Margalla — Taxila, Faisal Mosque, Pothohar
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — Swat, Kalam, Kalash Valleys
  • Sindh & the Coast — Karachi, Mohenjo-daro, Thatta

Afghanistan

  • Kabul — Old city, Babur’s Gardens, surroundings
  • Bamyan & Central Highlands — Band-e-Amir, Buddha valleys
  • Herat — Timurid monuments, Friday Mosque
  • Mazar-i-Sharif & Balkh — Blue Mosque, Silk Road sites
  • The Wakhan Corridor — Pamir landscapes, Kyrgyz nomads
  • Panjshir & Northern Valleys — gorges, mountain villages

What we take care of

Everything your journey needs, carefully handled.

From the first idea to the ride home, we manage the details that make travel across Pakistan and Afghanistan smooth, comfortable and genuinely yours.

  • Custom itineraries

    Routes, pacing and stays designed around your interests, dates and travel style.

  • Local guides

    Guides who live these regions — language, culture, context and the stories behind every place.
