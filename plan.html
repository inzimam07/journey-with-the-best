Good to know

Common questions, answered.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within 48 hours, usually faster. You can also WhatsApp us directly for a quicker conversation.

Is there a fee to request a proposal?

No. Proposals are free and come with no obligation.

Is travel in Pakistan and Afghanistan safe?

The regions we operate in are routinely visited by international travelers. We monitor conditions, coordinate with local partners and only propose routes we would travel ourselves. For Afghanistan specifically, every journey is reviewed carefully against current conditions.

Do I need a visa?

Pakistan offers an e-visa for most nationalities. Afghanistan requires a visa in advance. We’ll walk you through the right route and documents for your passport once we know who’s traveling.

What does a journey typically cost?

Every journey is private and tailored, so cost depends on route, duration, stays and group size. Share your rough dates and interests and we’ll come back with a transparent proposal.

Can plans change after booking?

Yes. If weather, flights or interests change on the road, we adjust in real time.
